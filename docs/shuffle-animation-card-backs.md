# Shuffle animation card backs

The synchronized shuffle proxy cards reuse the packaged card-back assets from `assets/cards-webp/other` through `catalog.backs`:

- general reserve: `back-generals.webp`
- identity reserve: `back-identities.webp`
- quick shuffle, draw pile, recycle zone, extra reserve, and marker reserve: `back-main.webp`

No duplicate image assets are introduced.
